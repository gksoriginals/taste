# Example Product design

Route from the user's task and affected surface. When reviewing account setup progress, use [the checklist](references/checklists/account-setup.md). When deciding how to show progress, use [the decision tree](references/setup-progress.md). This synthetic Pack does not define a general visual style or establish rules for other product surfaces.

Conformance applies established guidance. Expression explores a new direction or responds to a correction. These describe the current task, not Pack states. When feedback resolves into a clear reusable lesson, apply and self-check it, then update the relevant guidance with evidence. Keep one-off feedback in the current task.
