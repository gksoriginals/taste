# Example evaluation rubric

The harness runs a higher-capacity Eval Judge in a fresh context from the generator. Score each output from 1–5 on user-facing quality, task completion, guidance application, and alignment with supported product guidance. A 1 fails or conflicts with supported direction; 3 partly meets the task with material gaps; 5 meets it strongly without material gaps. Cite concrete output evidence for every score. Similarity must not reward copying a known flaw. Call out behavior that cannot be verified from the supplied files. Use product-owner judgments to calibrate this rubric before drawing broad conclusions; scores do not automatically edit the Pack.
